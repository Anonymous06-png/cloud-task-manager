## Why they are archived

These are individual foundation, Windows/PostgreSQL troubleshooting, early browser/UI drafts and interim audit/recovery notes. Main reports referenced by existing top-level documentation or kept verification evidence remain in docs/reports/ to avoid breaking internal links.

Do not run old commands blindly: reports may describe obsolete local paths or conditions.
